Privacy Policy

Effective: November 5, 2026

YutoImata ("we") sets out below how the iOS app "KIWA" (the "App") handles information.

The App does not collect any information about you. It has no accounts, no network access, no ads and no in-app purchases. It never asks for or reads your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or location.

1. Information we collect

We collect neither personal information nor usage information through the App, and we do not run any servers. The App does not connect to the internet and can be played offline.

2. What stays on your device

The App does not save your progress or settings. However, the game library it uses (DX Library) may write a diagnostic log, such as basic graphics information recorded at startup, to the App's private storage on your device. This log is never sent off your device and we cannot see it. It is removed when you delete the App.

3. What the App does not use

  • Advertising (including advertising identifiers and tracking)
  • Analytics or crash-reporting tools
  • In-app purchases
  • Accounts or any features that connect you with other users

4. Sharing with third parties

Because we collect no information, we have none to share.

5. Information you send us when you contact us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply and follow up. When no longer needed, we delete them.

6. Children

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Deleting data

We hold no user data on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete. To remove everything the App created on your device, including the diagnostic log, delete the App. If you want an email you sent us deleted, please contact us below.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App's features or applicable laws change. We will announce important changes on this page and in the App's update notes. The revised policy takes effect when it is posted here.
